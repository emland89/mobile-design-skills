# watchOS visual grammar

Use current Apple HIG for watchOS as authority.

- Design for glanceability and a wrist-scale focal hierarchy. One clear piece of information or action should usually dominate.
- Do not miniaturize iPhone cards, tab bars, toolbars, or dense dashboards.
- Use full-screen color, imagery, materials, and typography only when they improve immediate comprehension, identity, hierarchy, or sense of place.
- Respect the visual role of Digital Crown navigation, vertical scrolling/pagination, Always On, complications, widgets, and notifications.
- Keep controls visually large enough and sufficiently separated for wrist interaction; do not trade usability for visual density.
- Use motion and haptics economically: reinforce state and orientation without extending the interaction or demanding attention.
- Preserve product identity through a small number of strong semantic cues rather than reproducing the phone visual system at reduced scale.
