# UI review checklist

## Identity
- Is there a clear visual thesis tied to this product?
- Is there one memorable signature idea rather than many unrelated effects?
- Would the interface survive the logo-replacement test?

## Hierarchy
- Is the primary content/action dominant for the right reason?
- Are groups/relationships obvious without excessive containers?
- Do scale, alignment, spacing, and contrast agree on priority?

## Typography/color
- Are type roles coherent and scalable?
- Are color roles semantic and appearance-aware?
- Is contrast/accessibility validated?

## Shape/surfaces
- Does every container earn its boundary?
- Do materials/shadows communicate layer relationships?
- Are shapes/radii purposeful rather than mechanically repeated?

## Controls/platform
- Are conventional controls recognizable?
- Does branding preserve target-platform visual fluency?
- Does the screen adapt rather than merely stretch?

## Motion/haptics
- Does motion explain relationships/state?
- Is reduced motion supported?
- Are haptics meaningful and additive?

## Restraint pass
Remove the least necessary effect, border, shadow, card, accent, or animation. If the design becomes clearer or more distinctive, keep it removed.
