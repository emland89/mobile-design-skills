# Android visual grammar

Use current Material/Android guidance as authority; Material evolves.

- Material 3/Expressive offers adaptable color, type, shape, motion, and components; use them as a system, not as a requirement to copy Google's product aesthetic.
- Preserve recognizability of navigation, controls, selection, focus, and back behavior.
- Let adaptive layouts change composition and navigation across window classes.
- Use expressive shape/motion selectively to reinforce hierarchy and interaction rather than decorating every surface.
- Respect dynamic color/brand decisions intentionally rather than mixing them accidentally.
- Test font scaling, dark theme, accessibility contrast/focus, different windows, and pointer/keyboard where relevant.
