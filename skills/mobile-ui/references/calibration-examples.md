# UI calibration examples

These examples calibrate judgment. Do not copy their layouts or styling.

## Shared identity, different native expression

**Input:** Same finance product on iOS and Android.

**Good direction:** share semantic color roles, imagery, brand voice, data-visualization language, and a signature motif. Allow typography, controls, system icons, materials, component geometry, motion, and density to follow each platform's visual grammar.

## Watch visual scope

**Input:** The phone app has a 12-card analytics dashboard, but UX retains only a current-status glance and one quick action on watch.

**Good direction:** design those retained watch moments exceptionally well. Do not reconstruct miniature KPI cards to make the watch look like the phone.

## Tablet recomposition

**Input:** A reading app moves from iPhone to iPad.

**Good direction:** preserve the editorial identity while reconsidering readable measure, surrounding whitespace, supporting navigation, multiwindow behavior, pointer/keyboard feedback, and imagery scale. Do not proportionally enlarge all dimensions.

## Anti-template test

**Input:** "Premium AI app" with gradient hero and four rounded metric cards.

**Good critique:** first ask what the product's content and primary action actually are. Keep a gradient or cards only if they communicate a product-specific hierarchy or identity; otherwise replace the template structure rather than merely restyling it.
