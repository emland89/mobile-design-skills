# Iconography and imagery

## Iconography
- Use platform-standard symbols for conventional actions when their meaning is established.
- Pair ambiguous icons with labels.
- Keep stroke/weight/optical size coherent with surrounding typography and platform chrome.
- Avoid sprinkling icons beside every label as decoration.
- Custom iconography is most valuable for domain concepts, brand identity, or concepts the platform library does not express well.

## Imagery
- Images should communicate content, recognition, instruction, or atmosphere tied to the product.
- Prefer real/domain-specific imagery over generic decorative stock aesthetics.
- Respect cropping, safe areas, text legibility, dark appearance, localization, and accessibility descriptions.
- Do not use imagery to compensate for weak hierarchy.
