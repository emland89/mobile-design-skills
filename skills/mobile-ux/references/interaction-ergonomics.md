# Interaction and ergonomics

## Interaction contracts
Every important interaction should have:
- visible/discoverable affordance
- clear action semantics
- immediate acknowledgement
- perceivable changed state
- error/recovery path
- accessible alternative when gesture, motion, audio, or haptics are involved

## Touch and reach
- Size the interactive region, not merely the visible glyph.
- Give adjacent targets enough separation to prevent accidental activation.
- Put frequent actions where they remain reachable in the intended posture, but do not make thumb-zone diagrams a universal law; device size, handedness, posture, and accessibility vary.
- On tablets/foldables, ergonomics may favor rails, side controls, or pane-local actions over phone-style bottom placement.

## Gestures
- Prefer standard platform gestures.
- Do not make a custom gesture the only path to a core action.
- Make direct-manipulation gestures reversible where possible.
- Avoid gesture conflicts with system navigation and scrolling.
- Provide explicit controls for actions that would otherwise be hidden or difficult to discover.
